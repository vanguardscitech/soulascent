Soul Ascent Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Soul Ascent, package name com.gorkem.soulascent, handles data. Soul Ascent is an offline mobile game. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Data We Do Not Collect

Soul Ascent does not collect or transmit:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account information.
  • Precise or approximate location.
  • Photos, contacts, calendar entries, SMS, microphone, or camera data.
  • Advertising identifiers, analytics identifiers, or device identifiers.
  • Payment, financial, or health information.

Local Game Data

Soul Ascent stores gameplay data locally on your device so the game can remember progress. This may include high score, total score, coins, unlocked skins, selected skin, settings, daily or weekly quest progress, and local ghost replay data. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us or to a third party.

Device Sensors

If you enable tilt controls, Soul Ascent may read device motion sensor values such as gravity or accelerometer data during gameplay. This sensor data is used only on the device to control the character. It is not stored, transmitted, or shared.

Third-Party Services

Soul Ascent does not use third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, social login, cloud saves, or account services.

Data Sharing

We do not share personal data because Soul Ascent does not collect personal data.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete local game data by clearing the app data in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

Soul Ascent does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect personal data, no personal data from children is collected, transmitted, sold, or shared.

Security

Soul Ascent does not transmit user data over the internet. Local gameplay data is stored by the app on the device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes. Any update will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
